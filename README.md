# CalculatorDS

A feature-rich, high-performance **Citizen-style desk calculator** built with Flutter and GetX. CalculatorDS combines the tactile, logical flow of physical business calculators with advanced mathematical tools and modern glassmorphism styling.

## 🚀 Key Features

*   **Interactive Check & Correct (History Audit):** Easily navigate backward and forward through your step history (e.g., `CRT 01`, `CRT 02`). Switch to edit mode at any historical step to modify digits, swap mathematical operators, and watch the entire calculation cascade-recalculate dynamically.
*   **Sequential Left-to-Right Execution:** Mimics a physical desk calculator (e.g., `5 + 3 * 2` equals `16` instead of `11` using standard PEMDAS).
*   **Context-Aware Business Percentages:** Dynamic percentage adjustments for markups, discounts, and simple decimal multiplication/division.
*   **Math Utilities:**
    *   **Factors Screen:** Find all unique positive divisor integers (factors) optimized to $O(\sqrt{N})$ time.
    *   **LCM & HCF Screen:** Calculate Greatest Common Divisor (Highest Common Factor) and Least Common Multiple of lists of numbers.
    *   **Prime Factorization:** Decompose any number into prime factors instantly.
*   **Modern Premium UI & Controls:**
    *   Elegant **Glassmorphism** display layout.
    *   Visual theme customization with wallpapers.
    *   Toggleable **Light and Dark theme** modes.
    *   **One-Handed Mode** supporting keyboard scaling and alignment.
    *   Satisfying tactile vibration feedback.

This Privacy Policy applies to the **CalculatorDS** mobile application (referred to as the "App" or "Application") created for mobile devices.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**CalculatorDS does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device data, or usage metrics.**
